# VEV_4500 — Round 4 per-product lens battery

## Headline numbers
- Mids: d1 mean=748.4, d2=755.4, d3=739.2; std 14.6 / 17.0 / 18.6 (same scale as V_4000 — both inherit VE noise).
- Spread (a1-b1) median = **16** d1/d2, **15-16** d3 (IQR [15,16]). VE spread = 5. **V_4500 is the tightest-spread deep-ITM voucher** but still 3x worse than VE.
- Trades: 1 / 0 / 2 — virtually untraded by counterparties. No usable Lens D.
- TV: mean ≈0.008, median 0, std 0.76-0.79 — clean delta-1.
- **Same-tick β vs ΔVE (per day): d1=0.666, d2=0.660, d3=0.679** (R²≈0.35). Theoretical=1.0; β systematically lower than V_4000 (0.73-0.76).

## Lens A — what's surprising
Spread distribution is *almost* constant at 16 (5 ticks tighter than V_4000). One day-3 interval shows 15. Both modes coexist with identical level-2 occupancy (10000/10000).

## Lens B — what's surprising
ρ1 = −0.231 / −0.222 / −0.247 — *less* mean-reverting than V_4000 (which was −0.29). OU half-life 188-283. Variance ratio at k=200 = 0.46-0.56 — weaker mean-reversion than V_4000.

## Lens C — what's surprising
imb1 → Δmid_{t+1} β ≈ +8.0-8.5 / t≈+44; imb3 β ≈ −57 / t≈−47. Coefficients ~half the V_4000 magnitudes, consistent with the smaller spread. Wall-mid β ≈ 0.94 / t > 49.

## Lens D — what's surprising
Only 3 total trades across 3 days. Two are Mark 22 buy/sell, two are Mark 38 paired. The conditional Δmid is +3/+7 ticks but n=1 each — t-stat undefined. **No usable counterparty signal on V_4500.**

## Lens E (cross-asset with VE) — KEY
- Same-tick ρ(ΔV_4500, ΔVE) = +0.597 / +0.601 / +0.589 — essentially identical to V_4000.
- Lead-lag |ρ| ≤ 0.03 every k. **No tradeable lead-lag** (d2 k=+1 = +0.018 — within noise).
- **Residual AC1 after stripping β·ΔVE: −0.449 / −0.459 / −0.467** — strong, slightly weaker than V_4000.

## Lens F (weird)
std(ΔV_4500)/std(ΔVE) = 1.12 / 1.10 / 1.15 — voucher more volatile than VE again, but less so than V_4000 (1.25-1.32). Smaller spread → less rounding noise.

## Candidate alphas
- **R4-V4500-E01: β-residual fade.** Same mechanism as V_4000 but residual AC1 is slightly weaker (−0.46 vs −0.48) and spread is tighter (16 vs 21). Better candidate than V_4000 if any deep-ITM fade is to be tried. **Mag** = AC1×residual_std ≈ 0.46×0.55 ≈ 0.25 ticks/decision; need to beat 16-tick spread → VE remains cheaper to express VE views. **Falsifier**: shadow-mode net edge after crossing must be > 0.
- **R4-V4500-G01: synthesise via V_4500.** VE spread (5) << V_4500 spread (16), so VE is still cheaper. Parity violations 0/0/0 (tradeable arb side) / 1+1+2 on the wrong side. **Reject.**

## Rejected here
- Lead-lag k=±1 with VE: max |ρ|=0.018 — economically nil.
- Mark-conditioned trading: n=3 trades total / 30k ticks. No statistical power.
- IV-residual: TV ≈ 0 (median exactly 0 every day), no smile structure.
- Cross-strike spread V_4000−V_4500 vs (4500−4000)=500: residual would be cor(β1=0.74, β2=0.67) — mostly noise differential, would need its own panel.
