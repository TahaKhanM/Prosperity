# 10_experiment_logs

Purpose: keep the durable experiment registry plus dated working journals and
iteration notes that support but do not replace canonical stage artifacts.

Expected inputs: run manifests, hypothesis tags, strategy-family tags,
candidate-vs-baseline decisions and ad hoc working notes.

Expected outputs: `experiment_registry.csv`, `latest_experiment_summary.md` and dated working logs that can later be distilled into assumptions,
validation or diagnosis artifacts.

Naming conventions: keep the stable registry files at
`experiment_registry.csv` and `latest_experiment_summary.md`; use
`YYYY-MM-DD_<topic>.md` for supporting notes.

Classification: ancillary notes directory.
