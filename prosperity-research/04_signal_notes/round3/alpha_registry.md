### A1 — HYDROGEL soft-anchor market making

- **Product.** HYDROGEL_PACK (pos limit 200).
- **Hypothesis.** HYDROGEL has a slowly-moving mean (≈ 9 991) with
  ρ₁ = 0.997, OU half-life ≈ 300 ticks, Hurst ≈ 0.47. It is **not** rigidly
  pinned at 10 000 (only 13.8 % of ticks within ±5), so the playbook's
  `fair = 10000` hardcode underquotes on average and misses ~86 % of
  takes. Replace with `fair = clamp(EMA_200, 9980, 10010)`.
- **Tests ran.** Axis A (mean/median/std, rho1, half-life), axis C
  (imbalance signal), axis K (vol clustering ρ₁ = 0.087).
- **Result.** PASS — three days of stable statistics, no single-day spike.
- **Expected PnL (hist 3-day).** 4–8 k / day from takes + quotes, based on
  observed ~1 k takeable crossings / day and 1-tick edge.
- **Falsifier.** Live mean departs 9991 by more than 20 in the first 1 000
  ticks — switch to raw market-mid.
- **Sizing envelope.** Take up to min(book qty, 20) per ask/bid level at
  edge; quote ±4 around `fair` with soft skew at |pos| > 80.
- **Gates.** Passes all five (see `structural_quirks.md` §1; no mid-fill
  dependence; three-day plateau).
- **Rejection gates:** ship-now.

### B1 — VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT wall-mid adaptive MM

- **Product.** VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT (pos limit 200).
- **Hypothesis.** The bid/ask level with the largest resting volume (≥ 20)
  is a **leading** predictor of next-k-tick mid. (wall_mid − top_mid)
  regresses on Δmid_h with slope ≈ 0.77, t-stat ≈ 39 at h = 1.
- **Tests ran.** Axis B (cross-validated regression across 3 days),
  axis C (imbalance corroboration), axis K (weak clustering), axis J
  (lag-0 VE ↔ VEV corr 0.77).
- **Result.** PASS — coefficient stable across days (0.76, 0.79, 0.78) at
  h = 1. Half-life-consistent signal across h = 1, 5, 10 (all t > 6).
- **Expected PnL (hist).** 3–6 k / day — slightly lower than HYDROGEL
  because spread is 1 tick most of the time and takes are scarcer.
- **Falsifier.** Live h=1 correlation drops below 0.15 on first 2 k ticks.
- **Sizing envelope.** Fair = wall_mid; take at edge 1; quote ±3 around
  fair; soft skew at |pos| > 80.
- **Gates.** Passes all five.
- **Rejection gates:** ship-now.

### C1 — Order-book imbalance MM skew (k=2 top two levels)

- **Product.** HYDROGEL_PACK and VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT.
- **Hypothesis.** Signed top-2-level volume imbalance `(Σbid − Σask) /
  (Σbid + Σask)` at levels 1–2 predicts next-tick mid move with t ≈ 35
  (HYDROGEL) and t ≈ 20 (VE). Slope ≈ 12 and 3 respectively: a +0.25 imbalance
  predicts +3 (HYDROGEL) / +0.75 (VE) shells in one tick.
- **Tests ran.** Axis C across k ∈ {1, 2, 3} and h ∈ {1, 5, 10, 100}. k=3
  sign flips (level-3 quotes are adversarial), so only use k ≤ 2.
- **Result.** PASS. Stable across days (t-stats 31–35 on HYDROGEL, 19–21
  on VE for k=2, h=1). Falls off by h=100.
- **Expected PnL (hist).** 1–2 k / day as a quote-skew overlay on A1 and B1
  (additive, not independent).
- **Falsifier.** Live imbalance-predictor slope drops by 50 % in first
  1 000 ticks.
- **Sizing envelope.** Skew fair by `slope × imbalance` capped at ±2 ticks.
- **Gates.** Passes all five.
- **Rejection gates:** ship-now.

### G1 — Defensive parity-floor guard

- **Product.** All 10 vouchers.
- **Hypothesis.** Any tick where best_ask(K) + 1 ≤ best_bid(S) − K is a
  guaranteed-payoff arb (buy voucher, sell VE, hold to expiry).
- **Tests ran.** Axis H (corrected) — 0–2 events per 10 000 ticks on K ≤ 5000.
- **Result.** PASS structurally (no false positives), but **sparse** in

- **Expected PnL (hist).** < 100 seashells / day. Trivial.
- **Falsifier.** N/A — identity-based, cannot be wrong.
- **Sizing envelope.** Take up to 10 contracts per event; hedge 1-for-1
  in VE.
- **Gates.** Passes all (ultra-safe). Ship as a tiny guard behind feature
  flag `parity_guard = true`.
- **Rejection gates:** ship-now (priority low because PnL tiny).

### D1 — IV-residual scalp on near-ATM vouchers

- **Product.** VEV_5100, VEV_5200, VEV_5300, VEV_5400, VEV_5500.
- **Hypothesis.** Per-tick quadratic smile residuals `res_K = IV(K) − fit(m)`
  mean-revert with half-life 0.8–2.7 ticks. Trade against extreme residuals.
- **Tests ran.** Axis G (stat_tests on each strike's residual series),
  axis F (smile stability).
- **Result.** AMBIGUOUS — residuals mean-revert strongly (Hurst < 0.03) but
  σ ≈ 0.005 IV points × vega 10 = ~0.05 shells per event. To make 1 shell
  of edge we need 20+ residual-sigma deviations, which are rare.
- **Rejection gates.**
  - Single-day dependence: no (3-day stats match).
  - Concentration: yes — tradeable residuals cluster in a few dozen ticks
    per day. **Borderline gate 2.**
  - Parameter spike: plateau, ok.
  - Backtester mid-fill dependence: yes — naive backtest fills at mid and
    over-reports by ~2×. **Fails gate 4** until tight aggressive fills
    simulated.
  - Structural conflict: VEV_4000/4500 must be excluded (see quirk §2).
- **Priority.** research-more. Do not include in the first submission.
  Revisit once Rust backtester fill model tightened.
- **Falsifier.** Live residual |res − EMA(20)| never exceeds 0.003 over
  1 000 ticks → insufficient signal magnitude.

### E1 — Deep-ITM delta-1 overlay (VEV_4000 + VEV_4500)

- **Product.** VEV_4000, VEV_4500.
- **Hypothesis.** Because time value ≈ 0 on these strikes, holding
  1 long VEV_4000 to expiry pays `S_T − 4000` with probability ~1. Use them
  as **extra delta-1 capacity** beyond the 200 VE position limit.
- **Tests ran.** Axis E (time-value distribution), axis J (correlation
  0.91 with VE).
- **Result.** PASS structurally. But: 5-day live TTE means S could
  technically drop below 4000. Worst historical VE = 5236 (day 0 min ~5200),
  so ~1 200 shells of downside cushion. Probability S_T < 4500 in 5 days
  at σ=0.24 (ATM IV) and S_0 ≈ 5250: log-move required = log(4500/5250)
  = −0.154, σ_5d = 0.24 × √(5/365) = 0.028, z ≈ −5.5 → P ≈ 2·10⁻⁸.
  Not a binding risk.
- **Rejection gates.**
  - Single-day dependence: no.
  - Concentration: no — the overlay is a continuous holding, not a trade.
  - Parameter spike: ok.
  - Backtester mid-fill: **yes** — hosted liquidation vs backtester
    liquidation diverge. Gate 4 binding.
  - Structural conflict: this **is** the structural quirk; we exploit it.
- **Priority.** research-more. Promising but needs end-of-round settlement
  logic validated in the Rust backtester before shipping.
- **Expected PnL (hist).** 0–1 k / day if we fund the overlay at best_ask
  and ride drift. Much more if we use the overlay to hedge voucher shorts.
- **Falsifier.** Live best_ask(VEV_4000) − (S − 4000) exceeds 5 shells
  persistently → time value has returned, signal invalid.
